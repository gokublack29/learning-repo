Spot-the-Bug Drill: What happens if pos_logit = 120.0 and neg_logit = 118.0?

In naive Python, math.exp(120.0) works, but what if raw logits reach 800.0? How do you make 2-class softmax numerically stable?

Subtract m = max(pos_logit, neg_logit) before exponentiating: exp(pos - m) / (exp(pos - m) + exp(neg - m)). That is the log-sum-exp stability trick!
