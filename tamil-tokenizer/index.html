The full path

Five phases. The order matters: each one only makes sense once you have the one before it.

  1. Orientation Step 1 of 13, about 10 minutes

    Entry points and data flow

    What the program does, the three commands you actually run, and how text becomes token IDs. Everything else assumes this.

  2. Orientation Step 2 of 13, about 10 minutes

    Tamil orthography and conjunct stems

    Why Tamil needs its own tokenizer at all. The abugida, the pulli, the vowel signs, and why one base cluster costs three tokens without special treatment.

  3. Orientation Step 3 of 13, about 10 minutes

    Conjunct stems: the math and the vocabulary bloat

    The 528 stems that moved compression, and the measurement that rejected the 6,348 extra clusters. The most important trade-off in the repo.

  4. The pipeline Step 4 of 13, about 10 minutes

    Pretokenization and the Unicode regex

    Why merges must not cross word boundaries, and how the pretokenizer sets the walls.

  5. The pipeline Step 5 of 13, about 15 minutes

    Regex and Unicode properties from scratch

    What \p{L} and \p{M} mean, and a full walk through the Tamil pattern.

  6. The pipeline Step 6 of 13, about 10 minutes

    Regex quantifiers and grouping by example

    Plus, letters, braces and groups, with the one missing-pipe bug that broke the pretokenizer.

  7. The algorithm Step 7 of 13, about 12 minutes

    The BPE training engine and 64-bit pair packing

    The four phases of the training loop, and the three systems tricks that made it finish in seconds instead of hours.

  8. The algorithm Step 8 of 13, about 12 minutes

    Inference: priority merges, CSR layout, byte fallback

    What happens at encode time, why the merge table is shaped the way it is, and why nothing is ever dropped.

  9. The algorithm Step 9 of 13, about 10 minutes

    How this differs from the standard, and the zero-training base

    The five things this repo does that a general-purpose tokenizer does not, and what "works without training" actually means.

  10. The language Step 10 of 13, about 15 minutes

    The Rust you need to read this codebase

    Every type, operator and pattern in the repo, translated into terms you already know.

  11. The language Step 11 of 13, about 25 minutes

    Rust as a systems language

    Ownership, borrowing, memory layout, traits, and why the code packs bits into integers. This is the one that makes the rest click.

  12. Shipping Step 12 of 13, about 20 minutes

    The canonical model and the Hugging Face export

    How two engines ended up emitting identical token IDs, the promotion merge algorithm, and the export that writes five files.

  13. Capstone Step 13 of 13, about 45 minutes

    The interview dossier

    Every decision from all 130 commits with its evidence and commit hash, every measured number with its source, a stale-number trap list, and a five-minute demo script.

While you read, run these

# The base vocabulary, with no training at all
cargo run --release --bin vocabcount

# Train from the sample corpus
cargo run --release --bin train -- ./data/tamil_corpus.txt 50000 ./output/demo.json

# Encode, and watch the byte fallback
cargo run --release --bin encode -- ./output/demo.json "வணக்கம்! க்ஷ ஸ்ரீ 🎉"

# Export to Hugging Face and prove the two engines agree
cargo run --release --bin export_hf -- ./output/demo.json ./output/demo_hf
cargo build --release --bin encode_lines
.venv/Scripts/python.exe scripts/verify_hf_export.py ./output/demo.json ./output/demo_hf data/tamil_wiki_clean.txt 200

# Throughput, single thread against rayon
cargo run --release --bin bench -- ./output/demo.json data/tamil_wiki_clean.txt 16777216
