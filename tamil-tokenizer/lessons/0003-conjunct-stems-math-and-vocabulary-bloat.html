Lesson 0003

The 23 × 23 Math and Vocabulary Bloat

How the 528 conjunct stems are calculated, why vowel signs are excluded, and what happened when adding them was tested.

Step 3 of 13. Orientation. The 528 conjunct stems, and the measurement that rejected 6,348 more.

1. The Formula: Consonant + Pulli + Consonant

The 23 × 23 math is strictly two consonants joined by a pulli dot. Vowel signs are not in this list.

1
The 23 Consonants

18 traditional Tamil consonants + 5 Grantha consonants (ஜ, ஶ, ஷ, ஸ, ஹ) = 23 consonants.

2
The Combinations

Every consonant is paired with every other consonant with a pulli (்) in the middle:

first_consonant + ் + second_consonant
23 × 23 = 529 combinations
3
Deduplication: 529 - 1 = 528

The ligature க்ஷ (க + ் + ஷ) was already inserted earlier as a common compound character.

Because the code deduplicates via a hash set, 529 - 1 = 528 new unique tokens are added.

2. Why Vowel Signs Were Excluded

What if we also attached the 12 vowel signs (ா, ி, ீ, ு...) to every conjunct stem?

The Math of Vocabulary Bloat:

  • Bare stems: 23 × 23 = 528 tokens. Base vocabulary: 1,135 tokens.
  • Stems + vowel signs: 23 × 23 × 12 = 6,348 tokens. Base vocabulary: ~7,483 tokens.
The Real-World Benchmark Experiment

In vocab.rs:150-163, the author tested adding all 6,348 combinations.

The result was worse compression (bytes per token rose from 10.15 to 10.85).

Why? Most of those 6,348 combinations never appear in real life (for example, ழ் + ் + ங + ௌ). On a fixed 30,000 token budget, wasting 6,348 slots on impossible combinations steals room needed for common words like சென்னை, அரசு, or மக்கள்.

3. How Words with Vowel Signs Actually Get Handled

Take the word வண்டி (vandi = cart/vehicle):

  1. Initial base tokens: வ (consonant) + ண்ட (bare conjunct stem) + ி (vowel sign).
  2. During BPE training, the pair (ண்ட, ி) appears thousands of times in the training corpus.
  3. BPE naturally learns to merge them into ண்டி (Token 1329 in tamil_32k.json).

Result: Frequent clusters get merged by data evidence, while impossible combinations never waste a single token ID.
